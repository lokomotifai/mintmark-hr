## Fair community use

You may use the name to refer to this project truthfully: to say that your tool
reads Mintmark manifests, that you evaluated a detector on a dataset from this
pack, or that you wrote a tutorial about it.

You may not use the name or a confusingly similar name as the name of your own
product, in a way that suggests the project endorses you, or in a modified
distribution presented as the original.

## Attribution inside minted data

Every dataset carries its provenance in `MINTMARK.json`. That manifest is the
attribution mechanism and stays intact regardless of the dataset license.
Removing or forging it misrepresents where the data was struck.
